# DK Never Overcap RP 2.0.0

A rewrite of BDK Never Overcap RP. It now covers every Death Knight spec, has its own look, and
costs next to nothing.

- **All three specs.** Blood, Frost and Unholy, each on or off, each with its own color. The
  addon is now called DK Never Overcap RP.
- **New, hand-drawn visuals** instead of the game's spell alert textures. Around you: Rune circle,
  Halo, Arcs, Sigil or Embers. On the screen: Edge glow or Corners. Use one of each, or either
  alone.
- **Easy on the eyes.** Softer by default (70% max opacity, muted colors). Opacity glides with a
  short ease instead of jumping, and the near-cap pulse is a slow breath rather than a flash.
- **A new settings window**, flat and compact, with a live Preview. It opens from the minimap
  button or Options → AddOns, works in combat, and other UI skins leave it alone.
- **Light by design.** Nothing runs every frame: the addon reacts only to your runic power. On
  other classes it does nothing at all after login. Pattern textures load only when you pick them.
- **Quiet.** No chat messages and no slash commands.
- **Minimap button** that works with LibDBIcon when another addon has loaded it, so minimap skins
  and button bars treat it like any other launcher.
- Works in Midnight combat, where runic power can be hidden from addons.

Settings from 1.x don't carry over, because the addon has a new name.
