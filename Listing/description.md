**Never waste runic power again.**

As your runic power nears the cap, a soft glow builds around your character. It's faint when it
first appears and full at the cap, so you know to spend before it's wasted. No bars and no
numbers: you watch your character, and the glow tells you when.

## Highlights

- **Every Death Knight spec.** Blood, Frost and Unholy, each on or off, each with its own color.
- **Hand-drawn patterns.** Around you: Rune circle, Halo, Arcs, Sigil or Embers. On the screen:
  Edge glow or Corners. Use one of each, or either alone.
- **Easy on the eyes.** Soft by default. Opacity glides instead of flashing, and the near-cap
  pulse is a slow breath.
- **You choose when.** Set where it appears (85% by default) and when the pulse starts (95%).
  Optionally only in combat.
- **Preview** in the settings shows the overlay at full strength, any time.
- **Light and quiet.** Nothing runs every frame: it reacts only to your runic power, and does
  nothing at all on other classes. No chat messages.

## How to use

- Click the minimap button, or go to Options → AddOns → DK Never Overcap RP: the settings window.
- Right-click the minimap button to turn the overlay on or off.

Retail 12.1 (Midnight). No other addons needed.
